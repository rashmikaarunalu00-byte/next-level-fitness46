Next Level Fitness Tracker

Train smarter, eat to your goal and watch your progress build, all in one place.

Next Level Fitness Tracker is a simple, fast fitness companion that works on your phone or computer. Log every session, follow targets built around your own goal, and keep an eye on recovery so you can push hard on the right days. No clutter, just the numbers that help you improve.
What you can do
Your data
Your workout, nutrition and body-progress data are stored locally for quick access and synced to your own secure cloud record when Supabase is configured and the device is online. Download a backup from Profile regularly. Account passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never sent to Google. When you sign up or sign in, your name, phone number and email are also sent to the developer's Google Sheet (via Google Apps Script), as is any feedback you submit. Calorie, hydration, VO2 max and training figures are estimates, not medical advice.
Privacy lock

Protect the data stored on this device with a passphrase. It is asked every time the app opens, and your workouts, meals and sign-in are stored encrypted. If you forget it, the data on this device cannot be recovered (cloud-synced data can).
